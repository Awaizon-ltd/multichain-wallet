Security

Your keys never leave your device.

  • Encrypted on device. Your recovery phrase is sealed with your password (PBKDF2-SHA256 · AES-256-GCM) and auto-locks when you step away.
  • You approve everything. Every connection, signature and transaction opens Rizon's own approval window.
  • Plain-English warnings. Permits, unlimited approvals, look-alike domains and failing transactions are called out before you sign.
  • Exact-amount approvals. Swaps never grant a router unlimited access to your tokens.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Awarizon hold my funds?

No. Rizon is self-custody: your keys are created and encrypted on your device. Nobody — including Awarizon — can move your funds or recover your wallet, so keep your recovery phrase safe.

Which networks are supported?

Ethereum, Base, Arc, Robinhood Chain, Polygon, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Avalanche and Solana, plus the Base and Ethereum Sepolia testnets.

Can I use the same wallet on Android and desktop?

Yes. Import the same recovery phrase, or restore from your encrypted Google Drive backup, and you'll get the same accounts on both.

Will it conflict with MetaMask or Phantom?

No. Rizon never takes over other wallets — dApps that support modern wallet discovery list Rizon alongside the others, and you pick.

What does it cost?

Rizon is free. You pay normal network fees, and swaps include a small fee shown before you confirm.
