06 · Voice

Clear, calm, honest.

Clear before clever

Rizon moves people's money, so every word must be unambiguous. Say what will happen, not how exciting it is.

Calm and direct

Short sentences, active voice, plain English. Explain risk without fear.

Honest about self-custody

No "unhackable" or "100% safe". We say plainly that nobody, including Awarizon, can recover a lost phrase.

Say

  • You'll receive at least 1,280 USDC
  • This site wants unlimited access to your USDC
  • Swap · Send · Approve
  • Keep your recovery phrase offline

Not

  • Get the best gains instantly!
  • DANGER!!! Malicious contract
  • Click here · Submit · OK
  • Your funds are 100% safe
